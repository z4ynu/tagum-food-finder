## Status

Approved by the product owner on 2026-10-14.

The approved light direction covers the public place profile at mobile and desktop breakpoints:

- [Desktop Place Profile mockup](./place-profile-desktop.png)

## Direction locked for implementation

- The profile continues the approved light Discovery visual system and does not inherit a system-driven dark theme.
- Decision information appears before supporting detail: identity, category, distance, source state, price, hours, and dietary evidence.
- Missing information is stated beside the affected field rather than hidden or collapsed into one generic warning.
- Places without lawful listing media use an honest `No photo available` treatment.
- `Get directions` is the dominant action and remains sticky on mobile without obscuring content.
- Save, Share, Add to wheel, and Suggest an update remain secondary actions and may use prototype-only unavailable states until their phases are implemented.
- The location preview is supplemental; its address and destination remain readable without the map.
- The mobile profile is a focused drill-down route without the bottom navigation. Desktop retains the approved product rail.

## Prototype caveats

The map geometry, sample distance, source state, and marker placement in the images are illustrative. Implementation uses approved audit data, calculated distance, and the current evaluation map source. Generated imagery is not published as evidence about a real business.

Material visual changes require another owner review before implementation.
