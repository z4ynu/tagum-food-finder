# Draft: Tagum Food-Business Data Request

**To:** Business Permitting and Licensing Division, City Government of Tagum  
**Email:** `bplo@tagumcity.gov.ph`  
**Subject:** Request for non-personal food-establishment data for Tagum City feasibility research

Good day,

I am conducting a feasibility study for Asa Ta Mangaon?, a planned local food-discovery application focused on establishments within Tagum City. The study needs a representative evaluation sample and will not treat the requested data as authorization for production publication.

May I request the most recent available list of permitted customer-facing food and beverage establishments in Tagum City, preferably in CSV or spreadsheet format and limited to these non-personal fields where available:

- registered business or trade name;
- line of business or establishment category;
- public establishment address and barangay;
- active, expired, suspended, or closed permit status;
- most recent permit issue or renewal date;
- public establishment coordinates, if maintained by the city; and
- stable non-personal establishment or permit identifier, if one may lawfully be shared.

Please exclude proprietor, employee, and representative names; personal residential addresses; private telephone numbers or email addresses; taxpayer identifiers; identity documents; payment information; and other personal or confidential fields.

Could you also clarify:

1. whether the supplied fields may be used to evaluate and seed a public food-discovery directory;
2. required attribution, license, or terms of use;
3. whether public display of the business name, category, address, and operating status is permitted;
4. whether corrections and community-submitted updates may be stored alongside the city record;
5. the available update frequency and how later versions should be identified; and
6. any fees or formal request process required.

The project will retain source provenance, avoid publishing personal data, and document any restrictions before using the information outside the feasibility audit.

Thank you.
