# Reference Evidence Access

## Status

The 100-place sampling plan is locked, but no business has entered the reference sample yet. The first access review identified useful independent discovery sources, but none can alone satisfy the approved urban/rural, independent/chain, category, location, and current-status evidence requirements.

Provider coverage has not been measured and must not influence reference-place selection.

## Reviewed sources

| Source | Useful evidence | Limitations | Intended use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tagum Business Permitting and Licensing Division | Potential authoritative list of permitted operating establishments, business line, public establishment address, barangay, and permit status | Data is not published as a reusable food-business dataset; a scoped request is needed | Primary discovery frame if the city approves access and reuse |

| Gaisano Malls directory | Tagum tenant name, floor, and broad food category | Urban mall bias; no reliable branch coordinates or detailed food category on the directory | Candidate discovery, followed by branch-level verification |

| Official business sites or social pages | Identity, category, current activity, address, hours, and sometimes prices | Search indexing is uneven, especially in rural barangays; coordinates may be absent | Place-level evidence after independent candidate selection |
| Storefront observation or owner confirmation | Entrance coordinate, active status, branch identity, and missing category details | Requires local participation and must avoid recording personal information | Close evidence gaps that public sources cannot resolve |

## Findings

- DTI states that NegosyoKonek is public and contains registered business information, but DTI business-name registration is not an operating license.
- A prior DTI FOI response concerning Tagum microenterprises directs requesters to the city Business Permit and Licensing Office for operating-business information.
- The Tagum Citizens' Charter publishes the Business Permitting and Licensing Division contact route at `bplo@tagumcity.gov.ph`.
- Official mall directories can efficiently fill part of the chain quota but would badly bias the sample if used as its main discovery frame.
- Initial searches produced no sufficiently authoritative indexed food-place results for Bincungan, Busaon, Liboganon, or New Balamban. Third-party mirrors and recommendation pages are candidate leads, not final evidence.
- Exact entrance or building coordinates remain the largest web-only evidence gap. Address-only evidence cannot silently become a precise coordinate.
